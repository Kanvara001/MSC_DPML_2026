Three traps this page is built to expose

  • Accuracy lies under class imbalance. Drag prevalence to 1% and set the threshold high. A model that flags almost nobody scores above 98% accuracy while missing most of the disease. Report sensitivity, specificity and precision, or balanced accuracy, not accuracy alone.
  • Precision depends on prevalence, sensitivity and specificity do not. Hold the threshold still and move prevalence: sensitivity and specificity are frozen, because each is computed inside one row of the truth. Precision collapses, because at low prevalence the healthy majority contributes false positives faster than the sick minority contributes true positives. This is why a 95% sensitive, 95% specific screening test is still mostly wrong when the disease is rare.
  • Sensitivity and specificity are properties of a threshold, not of a model. One model gives you the whole ROC curve. Quoting a single sensitivity without its threshold, or without the specificity that came with it, describes one point and hides the rest.
